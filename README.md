## Devlog
"Components are the building blocks of GameObjects, and GameObjects are the building blocks of Scenes." (notes)


I think of a Scene as a complete house and GameObjects as its individual parts, such as bricks, roof tiles, doors, and windows. Components is the properties and functions of each parts. For example, a door needs size, position, and appearance that allows it to fit a house. In Unity, different components provide these features. Together, components define each GameObject, and the GameObjects come together to form the Scene.
